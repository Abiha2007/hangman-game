How scoring and difficulty work

Difficulty rule. Each word is tagged from its length, its number of distinct letters and rare letters (J Q X Z K V W Y). Longer words with more distinct and rarer letters are Hard. Attempts: Easy 8, Medium 6, Hard 5.

Score (win). (100 + 10 × distinct letters + 12 × attempts left) × multiplier (Easy 1, Medium 1.5, Hard 2). Score (loss). 5 × correct letters × multiplier.

Hint. One per game. Reveals a hidden letter, costs 25 points on a win, never costs an attempt. Daily challenge. Everyone gets the same word today and a x1.25 bonus.

Security. The answer stays on the server until the game ends, so it can not be found in the page source or network traffic.
